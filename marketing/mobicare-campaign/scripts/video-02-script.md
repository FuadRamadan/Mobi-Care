## Narration

Meet MobiCare. Licensed pharmacies near you, in one place.
Open it on your phone's browser at mobicaresl.com.
Search for the medicine you need.
Licensed pharmacies that stock it appear, nearest first, with the price and how many are in stock.
Compare brands, makers and where each was made, then add one to your cart.
Choose delivery by a MobiCare rider, or collect it from the pharmacy.
The delivery fee is shown before you pay, based on distance.
Then pay securely with Orange Money, AfriMoney, a card or a bank account.
And follow your order, from payment to the pharmacy preparing it.
Have a prescription? Take a photo and send it to pharmacies near you.
A pharmacist checks it and confirms what they have. You choose the quantities, then pay.
MobiCare. Search, order and follow your medicines. Discover it at mobicaresl.com.
